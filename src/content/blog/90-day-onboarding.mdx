## The $50,000 Mistake

Average cost to replace an employee: **$50,000** (recruiting, training, lost productivity, morale impact).

Most businesses hemorrhage this cost because they treat onboarding as:
- Day 1: Paperwork
- Week 1: "Shadow someone"
- Month 1: "Figure it out"

**This is organizational malpractice.**

## The 90-Day Protocol

I developed this for a multi-location car wash client drowning in turnover. Results:
- Turnover dropped from 80% → 25%
- Time-to-productivity cut in half
- Customer satisfaction scores increased 40%

### Phase 1: Days 1-30 (Foundation)

**Goal**: Build confidence through structured wins.

**Week 1: The Immersion**
- Day 1: Welcome ritual (team intro, culture alignment, vision casting)
- Days 2-3: Shadow top performers (learn by observation)
- Days 4-5: Hands-on with coaching (supervised execution)

**Week 2-4: Structured Repetition**
- Master ONE role completely
- Daily check-ins with manager
- Written feedback loop
- Celebrate small wins publicly

**Key Insight**: Don't overwhelm. Mastery of basics builds confidence for complexity.

### Phase 2: Days 31-60 (Expansion)

**Goal**: Build versatility and autonomy.

- Introduce adjacent roles
- Reduce supervision gradually
- Assign first independent project
- Begin peer mentorship

**The Critical Shift**: Employee stops being trained, starts being trusted.

### Phase 3: Days 61-90 (Integration)

**Goal**: Full team integration and ownership.

- Cross-functional exposure
- Leadership opportunities (even small ones)
- Input on process improvements
- Future growth path discussion

**The Alchemy**: By day 90, they're not just employees—they're stakeholders.

## The Psychological Framework

This isn't about skills. It's about **identity transformation**.

### Week 1-2: "I'm learning"
Vulnerable, uncertain, high risk of quitting.

### Week 3-6: "I'm contributing"
Confidence building, seeing impact.

### Week 7-12: "I'm essential"
Ownership, pride, belonging.

### Day 90: "This is my company"
Full psychological integration.

## The Documents You Need

1. **Culture Codex** (10 pages)
   - Mission, values, non-negotiables
   - Success stories, team culture

2. **Role Mastery Checklist** (1-2 pages per role)
   - Skills to master
   - Observable competencies
   - Sign-off from trainer

3. **30/60/90 Review Templates**
   - Structured feedback
   - Growth areas
   - Celebrations
